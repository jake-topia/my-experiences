How to Play

In Trickster Town, every player has a unique role with a unique action. This role belongs to either the Trickster team, or the Townsfolk team.

During the night time, every player will select another player to visit. **remove?**Visiting a player means you want to use your role's unique action on that player.****

There will always be at least 1 Trickster trying to eliminate another player and get away with it! The Trickster conducts an elimination by first visiting a player to "case" them. Then visiting again on another night to eliminate them.

The Trickster team wins if they eliminate a player and no Trickster team member is convicted of the crime.
The Townsfolk team wins if they successfully convict a Trickster team member of the crime.

TRICKSTER ~small subtext saying "Trickster Team": Case a player on Night 1, then eliminate that same player on a later night. Your case reveals their role to you.
WATCHER ~small subtext saying "Townsfolk Team": Watch a player to see who visits them.
FOLLOWER ~small subtext saying "Townsfolk Team": Follow a player to see who they visit. ****changed from Detective to Follower****
GUARD ~small subtext saying "Townsfolk Team": Guard a player to block all visits to them.
JOKER ~small subtext saying "Joker Team": Visit a player without affecting them. Win if you are put on trial and convicted.
MIND READER ~small subtext saying "Townsfolk Team": Prepare to read a player's role on Night 1, then read it on Night 2.
SABOTEUR ~small subtext saying "Trickster Team": Distract a player to stop them from using their action that night.
FRAMER ~small subtext saying "Trickster Team": Visit a player while appearing to be another player.
SLEEPER ~small subtext saying "Townsfolk Team": Visit a player to make them sleepy and stop their action.

**remove?**THIEF ~small subtext saying "Townsfolk Team": Swap roles with another player on Night 1. The swap takes effect after the night.****

Day/Night Cycle: Night 1 -> Day 1 -> Night 2 -> Day 2 -> Night 3 -> Day 3
Each role no longer has their own "phase" during the night. Everyone has a role now and everyone must DO their action during the night simultaneously. Things such as conflicts like the Guard preventing a visit needs to be resolved logically right after the night time voting is done. Those "blocking" actions means the player who was blocked/action-stopped actually visited no one and their action never happened. This may require a concrete order of blockage, trickster based blockage such as the Saboteur should be prioritized first.
The exile phase should be changed to a trial phase. This phase only happens the day after an elimination occurs. The new trial phase should function like so: The players vote who to put on trial and while on trial the accused can try to convince the town otherwise, after some time the players can vote again to "Put Someone Else on Trial" OR "They're Guilty!" -> these options will appear with their own vote text sprites underneath them below the accused person. The accused person will be teleported to the middle of the circle while on trial. Players can "Put Someone Else on Trial" as long as they want. The first 5 roles under "Roles:" should always be a part of the game as of now.

New win condition: The trickster team has 3 nights to eliminate a player, if the day after night 3 (day 3) a trickster is put on trial and convicted, the tricksters lose. Due to the trickster's ability they can eliminate as early as night 2, if this happens an early trial will happen on day 2 - again, if a trickster is convicted, they lose. If no one is eliminated by Night 3 or a trickster is correctly convicted after trial, the Townsfolk team wins. Logically, trial always happens immediately the next day if there was an elimination the night before. The Joker's win can happen simultaneously with the trickster team (the Joker is convicted instead of a trickster ).

Roles:

- Detective (team townsfolk): can investigate to see where a player went, investigating means this player left their house targeting the investigated player. 

- Watcher (team townsfolk): can watch a player house to see if they get visited and by whom, watching means this player left their house targeting the watched player. 

- Trickster (team Trickster): can case a player, casing means this player left their house targeting the cased player (1st night, casing must happen before elimination). Can eliminate a player, eliminating means this player left their house targeting the eliminated player (2nd night, you may eliminate only the player you cased). Wins if they do not get exiled after the 2nd night. The trickster "case" also reveals the cased players role to the trickster

- Guard (team townsfolk): can guard a player, guarding means this player left their house targeting the guarded player. Guarding a player means this player is protected from being visited by another player entirely (player attempting to visit is not revealed to the guard). Post-night text should say something like "You guarded <player> against 3 visitors"

- Joker (team joker): can visit a player (does nothing), visiting means this player left their house targeting the visited player. Wins if they get exiled.
