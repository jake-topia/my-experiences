Trickster Town is a multiplayer game where you are assigned a role and you have to work with or against other players to win!

Tricksters are the impostor role and have time during the night to eliminate players, they win when enough normal players (Townsfolk) are eliminated

Townsfolk (including adjacent roles such as the Detective and the Doctor) win by voting together to exile suspected Tricksters during the daytime.

Special roles can also act during the night to use their abilities. For example, the Detective may investigate to reveal if a player is a Trickster.
